Carcosa

RUNNING
Extract the entire ZIP, then open bin/Carcosa.exe.
Keep bin, assets, config, and shaders together in the extracted folder.
Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later and a Vulkan-capable graphics driver.
The Vulkan SDK and development tools are not required.

CONTROLS
WASD - Player movement
LMB on enemy - Melee attack
Close the window to quit. Restart the game to reset after death.

FUNCTIONALITY
- Player movement with a following camera.
- Enemy AI that chases and attacks the player.
- Health, damage, and death mechanics.
- Data-driven enemy horde spawning.
